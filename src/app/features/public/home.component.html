About Training & Teacher Connection

Training & Teacher Connection (TPC) is a smart platform designed to bridge the gap between learners and professional trainers. Our goal is to make quality education accessible, flexible, and location-friendly.

Whether you are looking for academic tutoring, professional skills, technical training, or child-focused coaching, our platform helps you discover verified trainers near you.

Our Mission

To simplify access to quality training by connecting learners with trusted and verified instructors in their local area.

Our Vision

To become the leading door-to-door training and education connection platform in Ethiopia and beyond.
